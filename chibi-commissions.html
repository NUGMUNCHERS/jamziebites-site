Where people use them

  • Custom emoji and stickers for your Discord server or Telegram.
  • Profile pictures and icons that actually look like your OC.
  • Badges, prints and little gifts for friends (personal use).
  • Reaction images that are 100% you.
Can I use my stickers on Discord and Telegram?

Yes! Using them as stickers, emoji and icons is exactly what they're made for. Selling them or using them for a brand needs a commercial license.

How many expressions are in a pack?

One per sticker: a 5 pack is five different stickers, a 10 pack is ten. Tell me the emotions or poses you want, or let me surprise you.

How long do chibis take?

Usually on the quicker end of my 2–12 weeks turnaround, depending on my queue.

Do I need a reference sheet?

Yes, I need one for every commission so your character comes out right. Don't have one? I draw those too!
