What makes a piece cinematic?

It's a complete scene, not just a character with a background: a real environment, mood lighting, depth and atmosphere, all fully rendered.

How long does a cinematic piece take?

These take the most time, usually toward the longer end of my 2–12 weeks turnaround. Rush is +35% when I have room.

Can I add more characters?

Yes! Each extra character is 85% of the base price. The quote calculator shows the total.

Can I print it?

Yes, prints and merch for personal use are welcome. Selling them needs a commercial license (+100%).
