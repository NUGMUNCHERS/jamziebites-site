What I draw

  • Fursonas, OCs and furry characters: canines, foxes, dragons, sharks, bats, hybrids and more.
  • Soft, glowy coloring with sparkles, pastel tones and moody night and space lighting.
  • Anything from a cute chibi or sticker pack to a full scene with a detailed background.
  • Reference sheets for new and existing characters, including custom character designs.
How do I commission you?

Fill out the request form with your character's ref sheet and what you'd like. I'll look it over and get back to you by email or Discord. You only pay after I accept, through a private page with your exact price.

How much does furry art cost?

Stickers start at $15, chibis at $25, headshots at $35, half body at $50 and full body at $75. Ref sheets start at $120 and cinematic illustrations at $190. The quote calculator adds up shading, backgrounds and extras for you.

Can you draw my fursona or OC?

Yes, that's exactly what I do! Send me your character's reference sheet so I get every marking and color right. No ref sheet yet? I draw those too.

Do you draw NSFW?

Yes, for a flat +$95, discussed with me beforehand. Clients and all characters involved must be 18+, and I can decline anything I'm not comfortable with.

How long does a commission take?

Usually 2–12 weeks, depending on the size of the piece and how busy my queue is. Rush is +35% when I have room.

Is your art AI generated?

Never. Every piece is drawn by hand, by me. My art also may not be used to train AI.
