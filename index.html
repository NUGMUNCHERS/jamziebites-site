                Frequently Asked

                Are commissions open right now?

                Check the badge at the very top of the page. It always shows whether commissions are open, waitlist-only, or closed, plus how many slots are left this round.

                How do I pay?

                Once your commission is accepted and I've confirmed your final price, pay it on the Pay page by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay (processed securely by Stripe). Full payment (100%) is required up front before any artwork is started. I don't take deposits, partial payments or payment plans. Your slot is secured once payment arrives.

                Please don't send payment until your commission has been accepted.

                How long will it take?

                Usually 2–12 weeks depending on complexity, workload and life. Larger projects can take longer. Deadlines aren't guaranteed unless we agree on one in writing. Need it sooner? Rush commissions are +35% when I have room.

                How many revisions do I get?

                Two minor revisions are included during the sketch stage. Additional revisions or major changes after sketch approval may cost $10–$50+, and a complete character change may count as a new commission. If I make a mistake, I'll fix it for free!

                Do you draw mature / NSFW content?

                Yes! NSFW is a flat +$95 on top of the commission price. It must be discussed with me beforehand, and clients and all characters involved must be 18+. I may decline any subject matter I'm not comfortable with.

                What do I need to send you?

                A character reference sheet is required for every commission, plus any specific requests (pose, expression, outfit, background, vibe). Clear, accurate and up-to-date, please!

                My character doesn't have a ref sheet / isn't designed yet. Can you help?

                Yes! Order a Custom Design Ref Sheet: a Simple or Deluxe ref sheet plus the custom character design add-on (+$50). Custom designs are only available as a ref sheet, not as part of a scenic piece or illustration. Once your character has a ref, you can commission anything else!

                Do you do art trades?

                Art trades are currently open! Send a request with “Art Trade” as the type and a link to your own art, and we'll work out the details.

                Can I commission art of someone else's character?

                Only if you have the owner's permission. Gifts are lovely, just make sure it's okay with them first!

                Can I use my commission as a PFP, banner or print?

                Yes! Profile pictures, banners, wallpapers, character galleries and prints/merch for personal use are all welcome. Just credit JamzieBites when you post it. Selling it, monetizing it or using it for a brand needs a commercial license (+100%).

                What counts as commercial use?

                Merch sales, monetized content, streaming assets, business branding, ads, games, publications and other for-profit uses. Commercial licensing starts at +100% of the commission price; larger projects may need a separate agreement. It doesn't transfer copyright or allow edits, AI use or NFTs.

                Can I edit or recolor the art, or have another artist finish it?

                No, please don't. That includes tracing, drawing over, recoloring, adding/removing features, animating, or removing my watermark. Owning the character doesn't grant permission to alter my illustrations. Just message me and I'll happily make changes for you!

                Can I use the art with AI or for NFTs?

                Never. That includes uploading it to generative AI tools, AI “enhancements” or variations, training datasets, style replication, and minting or selling NFTs. That goes for commercially licensed pieces too.

                Can my commission be private or anonymous?

                Yes! You can ask to be listed anonymously or for a delayed public reveal. Fully private commissions (never posted) need to be discussed first and may have an extra fee.

                What's your refund policy?

                Full refunds are available if I haven't started yet. Once work has begun, refunds depend on the time and progress invested, and completed portions aren't refundable (except where required by law). If I cancel and can't finish your piece, you'll get a full refund. See the Terms for the details.

                Will I get the PSD / layered file?

                Finished art is delivered digitally in high resolution, with a transparent version when applicable. PSDs and layered files aren't included unless we agree on it separately. Please keep your own backups, since I can't store files forever.

                Can you draw in another artist's style?

                Sorry, no. I only draw in my own style (which may naturally evolve over time!).
