good to know

Why get a ref sheet?

  • Every artist you commission later can draw your character right the first time.
  • Fursuit makers ask for one before they start a suit.
  • It's one clear picture to share instead of a pile of scattered refs.
  • I need one for any other commission anyway, so it's the perfect first piece!
How long does a ref sheet take?

Usually 2–12 weeks, depending on how detailed it is and how busy my queue is. Rush is available for +35% when I have room.

Can you design a brand new character for me?

Yes! Add the custom character design option (+$50) to a Simple or Deluxe sheet, tell me your ideas, and I'll design your OC from scratch.

Can I share my ref sheet with other artists and fursuit makers?

Absolutely, that's what it's for. Just keep my signature on it and credit JamzieBites when you post it.

How do I pay?

Only after I accept your request. You'll get a private page with your exact price and a secure pay button (card, Apple Pay or Google Pay).
