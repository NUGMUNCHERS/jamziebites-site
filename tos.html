1General Terms

  • I reserve the right to accept, decline, or cancel any commission in accordance with my refund policy.
  • All artwork is created in my own artistic style. I will not replicate another artist's style.
  • I retain artistic freedom regarding anatomy, composition, lighting, and stylistic interpretation. My style may naturally evolve over time.
  • Harassment, discrimination, manipulation, threats, and disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.
  • I reserve the right to refuse any subject matter that makes me uncomfortable or promotes hatred, exploitation, discrimination, or illegal activity.
  • Mature or sensitive content must be discussed beforehand. Clients and characters involved in adult-themed commissions must be 18+.

2Payment & Pricing

  • All prices are listed in USD.
  • Full payment (100%) is required upfront before any artwork is started.
  • Deposits, partial payments, and payment plans are not accepted.
  • Commission slots are secured once full payment is received.
  • Prices may increase depending on complexity and additional requests.
  • Additional charges must be paid before the requested work begins.
  • Rush commissions are subject to a 35% additional fee and availability.
  • Do not send payment until your commission has been accepted.

3References & Revisions

  • Clients must provide clear, accurate, and updated references, including any specific requests.
  • You must have permission to commission characters belonging to others.
  • I am not responsible for inaccuracies caused by incomplete or outdated references.
  • Written descriptions without visual references may incur a design fee.
  • Two minor revisions are included during the sketching stage.
  • Additional revisions or major changes after sketch approval may cost $10–$50+.
  • Complete character changes may be considered a new commission.
  • Mistakes made on my behalf will be corrected free of charge.
  • Changes requested after completion may incur additional fees.

4Turnaround & Communication

  • Estimated turnaround time is 2–12 weeks, depending on complexity, workload, and personal circumstances. Larger projects may take longer.
  • Deadlines are not guaranteed unless agreed upon in writing.
  • Work-in-progress updates will be provided at appropriate stages, and significant delays will be communicated.
  • Reasonable update requests are welcome; excessive messaging, pressure, and rushing are not.
  • After 14 days without a response, your commission may be placed on hold.
  • After 30 days of inactivity and reasonable contact attempts, your commission may be considered abandoned and subject to my cancellation policy.

5Refunds & Cancellations

  • Full refunds are available if artwork has not been started.
  • Once work has begun, refunds are determined by the time, labor, and progress invested.
  • Completed portions of artwork are nonrefundable, except where required by law.
  • Completed commissions are nonrefundable for changes of mind or previously approved artistic decisions.
  • If I personally cancel and cannot complete your commission, you will receive a full refund.
  • Refunded, canceled, or unpaid artwork may not be used, posted, edited, or distributed without permission.
  • Fraudulent chargebacks and knowingly false payment disputes may result in permanent blacklisting.
  • All refunds remain subject to applicable law.

6Copyright & Usage Rights

I retain the copyright and intellectual property rights to all artwork I create unless otherwise agreed upon in writing. Purchasing artwork does not transfer copyright. You retain ownership of your original character.

As the artist, I reserve the right to display commissions, progress images, and speedpaints on my social media, portfolio, advertisements, and promotional materials.

Clients are permitted to:

  • Repost artwork with proper credit to JamzieBites.
  • Use artwork as profile pictures, banners, and wallpapers.
  • Display artwork in personal character galleries.
  • Produce prints or merchandise strictly for personal use.
  • Share references with other artists and fursuit makers.
  • Transfer artwork alongside character ownership, provided these terms remain intact.

7Unauthorized Editing & Alterations

Modifying, altering, or adding to my artwork without my explicit written permission is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Drawing, painting, or tracing over my artwork.
  • Adding or removing markings, accessories, clothing, or features.
  • Recoloring, shading, rendering, or applying filters.
  • Altering anatomy, expressions, proportions, or backgrounds.
  • Animating, rigging, or manipulating my artwork.
  • Completing unfinished artwork or sketches.
  • Editing or expanding reference sheets.
  • Removing, covering, or altering my signature or watermark.
  • Hiring another artist to edit, finish, or add to my artwork.

Owning the depicted character does not grant you permission to alter my artwork.

Minor cropping and resizing for personal display are permitted, provided my signature remains intact and the artwork is not misrepresented.

You are welcome to redesign your character or commission independent artwork of them, but you may not directly alter my existing illustrations.

Please contact me directly if you would like any changes or additions.

8AI & NFT Policy

My artwork may not be used for artificial intelligence, machine learning, or NFT-related purposes. This includes:

  • Uploading artwork into generative AI programs.
  • AI training, datasets, and style replication.
  • AI-generated variations, enhancements, animations, or alterations.
  • Creating, minting, distributing, or selling NFTs.
  • Unauthorized blockchain-related projects.

This applies to all sketches, reference sheets, unfinished and completed artwork, including commercially licensed commissions.

9Commercial Usage

  • All commissions are for personal use unless commercial rights are purchased.
  • Commercial usage includes merchandise sales, monetized content, streaming assets, business branding, advertisements, games, publications, and other for-profit activities.
  • Commercial licensing starts at an additional 100% of the commission price.
  • Larger projects may require a separate licensing agreement.
  • Commercial rights do not transfer copyright or authorize alterations, AI usage, or NFTs.

10Reference Sheets & Character Designs

  • Reference sheets may be shared with other artists but may not be edited, expanded, or recolored without permission.
  • Additional views, expressions, outfits, accessories, and redesigns will be charged separately.
  • Custom character designs may be resold, traded, gifted, or transferred by their rightful owner.
  • Original design and artwork credit must remain intact.
  • Character ownership does not transfer copyright to my illustrations.

11Privacy & File Delivery

  • Anonymous commissions and delayed public reveals may be requested.
  • Fully private commissions must be discussed beforehand and may incur an additional fee.
  • Personal client information will remain private unless disclosure is necessary for payment processing or legal obligations.
  • Finished artwork will be delivered digitally in high resolution, with transparent versions when applicable.
  • PSDs, editable layers, and original project files are not included unless separately agreed upon.
  • Outstanding additional charges must be paid before final delivery.
  • Clients are responsible for backing up their artwork. Indefinite file storage is not guaranteed.

12Violations & Final Agreement

Violating my Terms of Service may result in commission cancellation, refusal of future services, permanent blacklisting, removal requests, DMCA takedowns, or legal action where applicable.

Failure to immediately enforce a term does not waive my rights.

By submitting payment after receiving these terms, you acknowledge and agree to my Terms of Service, whether or not you have read them in their entirety.

I reserve the right to update these terms for future commissions unless otherwise mutually agreed upon.

Please respect my time, creativity, boundaries, and intellectual property.
